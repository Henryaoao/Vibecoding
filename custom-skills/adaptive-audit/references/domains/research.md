# Research Standards Pack

Use this pack when the artifact is a research memo, analysis, recommendation, or decision brief.

Apply this pack in addition to `references/common-checklist.md`.

## 1. Question Framing

Check:

- the question is explicit
- scope is bounded
- success criteria are clear

## 2. Evidence Quality

Check:

- claims are supported
- evidence is current enough
- uncertainty is acknowledged
- inference is separated from fact

## 3. Recommendation Strength

Check:

- recommendations follow from evidence
- tradeoffs are visible
- hidden assumptions are surfaced
- operational implications are named

## 4. Source Discipline

Check:

- sources are appropriate
- weak evidence is not overstated
- stale evidence is not presented as current
