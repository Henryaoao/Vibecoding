# Output Template

## Task Classification

- trivial / standard / substantial / critical
- why this depth is correct

## Domain Target

- selected domain standards family
- why the learning belongs there or why it should become cross-domain

## Evolution Conclusion

- what happened
- whether learning exists
- whether change is warranted

## Loop 1 Target Artifact

- what should change
- why that target is correct

## Loop 2 Meta-Optimization

- whether the evolution process showed weakness
- what part of the evolution system should improve, if any

## Generalized Principle

- the principle
- why it generalizes
- why it is not just a local case

## Anti-Overfit Review

- what diversity exists
- what overfit risk remains

## Recommended Action

- no change
- log only
- candidate
- promote
- patch / minor / major proposal

## Next Steps

- record or intentionally do not record
- review
- propose release
- defer
