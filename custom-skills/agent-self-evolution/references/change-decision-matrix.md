# Change Decision Matrix

Use this matrix after abstraction and anti-overfit review.

## No Change

Choose this when:

- the post-task gate found no reusable learning
- the lesson is local only
- confidence is low
- evidence is weak

## Log Only

Choose this when:

- the lesson is interesting
- the principle is plausible
- not enough diverse evidence exists yet

## Candidate Principle

Choose this when:

- the lesson is generalized
- the impact is meaningful
- promotion would still be premature

## Promote Into Active Rule

Choose this when:

- the principle has repeated across sufficiently diverse contexts
- failing to promote would keep causing avoidable mistakes

## Patch Change

Choose this when:

- the core behavior is stable
- scripts, wording, examples, or integration wording need tightening

## Minor Change

Choose this when:

- a new capability, dimension, integration contract, or reusable principle is added
- a host-skill integration requirement becomes explicit across reusable agents

## Major Change

Choose this when:

- the operating philosophy changes
- the mandatory gate or evolution gates change materially
- the skill's expected outputs or core workflow shift substantially
