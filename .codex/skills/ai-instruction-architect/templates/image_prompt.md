## Iteration Plan

1. Generate a base image.
2. Adjust one or two variables only.
3. Preserve composition or subject identity explicitly if needed.
4. Record the best prompt and any revised prompt returned by the model.

## Review Checklist

- Subject is unambiguous.
- Composition is inspectable.
- Lighting/material details are concrete.
- Text/logo constraints are explicit.
- No conflicting style or camera instructions.
